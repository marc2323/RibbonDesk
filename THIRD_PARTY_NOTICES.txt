RibbonDesk's AppKit gesture handling, Mission Control observation, and temporary
AXEnhancedUserInterface animation flag handling were informed by Paneru's macOS
implementation. RibbonDesk contains its own Swift implementation, with no Paneru
Rust binary or Bevy runtime. The upstream notice is retained below.
